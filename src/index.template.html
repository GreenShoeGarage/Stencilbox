What the checks mean

Topology, volume and clearances are computed from the actual export geometry. Strength, flexibility, adhesion and drawing feel are not tested.

The STL is a flat, constant-thickness extrusion, at Z = 0. Interpret its coordinates as millimeters. Review the sliced layers before printing.

Evidence & assumptions

Good to know

A stencil needs connected material. Openings that cross the safe border or violate the minimum web are left out. Some pattern families generate approximate counts; the usable count is always shown.

Whole-pattern transforms can change which openings fit. Individual edits are rejected if unsafe. Generating a different family or seed clears individual edits; Undo brings them back.

Pattern scale scales positions and openings. Opening size scales each cutout around its own center. Advanced mode adds stretch, shear, translation, mirrors and print assumptions.

On paper shows the ideal geometric outline. Real marks also depend on pencil shape and stencil thickness. Curves are approximated with line segments.

Local autosave belongs to this browser and location. Export JSON for a durable backup or to share. This app does not upload anything, fetch libraries, use analytics, or require an account.
